SPROUT

Sound Garden on the FM-1, in the browser: the same code the FM-1 runs

The garden (level 1)

  • Play the keys: the key lights on the screen with its name, and low, middle or high under it.
  • Turn KNOB 1 right for the garden: a gentle bed to play over (every white key fits; the key the bell is on glows). Left for free play.

The grown-ups' menu

  • Hold GLO and HOME together for a second and a half (here: Shift-click both, then let go).
  • SELECT moves, KNOB 1 changes, SEL opens, HOME goes back.
  • The level and what says the child is ready for the next; the note names (letters, do re mi, hidden); SMALL AXE.

SMALL AXE

  • A dub desk on a riddim: black keys 1–8 drop a channel (tap: out or in; hold: out while held), white keys 1–8 throw it into the echo, 9–16 into the spring, black keys 9–11 the siren.
  • KNOBS 1–4 the echo's time, repeats and tone and the spring's size; SELECT the tempo; PLAY plays.
  • HOME opens the workshop: LOOM-8's own pages on the same riddim. The grown-ups' menu comes back.

Keyboard

  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–11: 1–0, -.
  • Esc HOME, Space PLAY, ↑ ↓ turn SELECT. Drag a knob, or scroll over it.

Saving

The garden and SMALL AXE keep their projects in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps them in its memory. SPROUT is built on LOOM-8 by Charles Vestal; running FM-1 firmware in the browser comes from Groove OS's emulator.
