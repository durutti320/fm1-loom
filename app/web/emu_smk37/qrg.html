REAPER / DAW mode's REAPER profile

Reverse · SPIDER on the M-VAVE SMK-37, branch spider2 (beta), build of 6 November 2026 · Mackie Control for REAPER's own control surface

Try it first in the emulator: < + >, PATCH held + K6 to REAPER, then Pretend REAPER

Turn it on

1< + >: DAW mode (it starts in DAW mode if left there)
2PATCH held, K6 (CC MAP) to REAPER: DEFAULT, USER, REAPER, a detent a step. Kept
3Waiting for REAPER until REAPER answers. Every 3 s of silence SPIDER asks it to send everything again

Two USB MIDI ports

1 · musicSPIDER SMK-37: keys (CHANNEL, 1 to start), drum pads (ch 10), wheels, the NAV knobs (ch 16), clock. As DEFAULT; LOCAL as always
2 · controlWindows: MIDIIN2 / MIDIOUT2 (SPIDER SMK-37); macOS, Linux: SPIDER DAW. Mackie Control both ways; never plays SPIDER

Set up REAPER (once)

1Preferences → Audio → MIDI Devices: SPIDER SMK-37 in on (and for control messages), out on. MIDIIN2 / MIDIOUT2: leave off here
2Preferences → Control/OSC/web → Add → Mackie Control Universal: in MIDIIN2, out MIDIOUT2 (SPIDER SMK-37). Map F1–F8 to markers: off
3Actions → Show action list: pick an action, Add…, turn the NAV knob or press the ACTIONS pad. K2–K5: mode Relative 2
4Tracks you record: input SPIDER SMK-37, channel 1 (keys) or 10 (pads), not all channels, so NAV's ch 16 stays out of takes

SPIDER in time with REAPER

SetOut of DAW mode, CLOCK on receive (GLOBE, Setup; the default). REAPER's MIDI Devices, the SPIDER SMK-37 out: Send clock (and SPP)
ThenPlay from anywhere in REAPER: SPIDER starts each track at the step it would be on there, and the bar count with it
SPIDER's side: app/smk_mcu.h · REAPER's: its SDK's reaper_csurf/csurf_mcu.cpp · every detail: SMK37.md, The REAPER profile Two-sided: flip on the short edge
