8 Knobs

K1Volume (the FM-1's MASTER); the screen shows the level
K2SELECT · the button's next page; in a list, up / down
K3ALGORITHM · picks the track: T1–T8, COND, THROW, WEAVE
K4PRESETS · the next preset, drum key, effect, pattern or scene
K5–K8KNOB 1–4 · the four values along the bottom of the screen
On Steps (SEQ)
Nothing heldK5 pattern · K6 length · K7 scale · K8 groove
A pad heldK5 velocity · K6 length · K7 chance · K8 pitch
Pad + ARPK5 trick 1 · K6 its value · K7 trick 2 · K8 its value

4 Faders + 2 Wheels

Faders 1–4Levels of T1–T4; after FADER BANK (lit), T5–T8. A fader acts once it moves
Pitch wheelBends the track the keys play, up to a fifth (±7 semitones)
Mod wheelThat track's filter cutoff (DYNAMIC filter: its hi cut). Picks up: acts once it passes the track's value

37 Keys

F3–G5The FM-1's 27 keys, with velocity (GLOBE, Setup: KEY VELOCITY). White 1–16 are their white keys from F3, black 1–11 their black keys
Outer 10C3–E3 and G♯5–C6: more notes on the same track; on a kit, its keys
On a kitBlack 1–11: its eleven drum sounds (black 1 the kick)
ArrangeWhite: scenes 1–16 (OCT+: 17–32). Black: mutes
ThrowsFX, K2 to Throws. Hold a white key, F3–E4 on the drums, F4–E5 on the melodic tracks: mute · stutter · repeat · echo · build · floor · jumble. Black keys: warble · pan · octave · chop · ring

Buttons

SMK-37FM-1 name · what it does
PATCHHOME · Arrange; again: Song. Held: a shift (undo, redo, tempo). To a question: no
BTSAVE · saves now (lit: unsaved). Held + white 1–2: to that slot. On a sound page, held + a white key: keep the sound
PARAEDIT · the sound: Engine, Play, Play 2 (K2 turns the pages)
CHORDENV · Envelope, Filter, Filter envelope
FXFX · Character, Sends, FX I, FX II, Throws
SCALESEL · the page's settings and actions. To a question: yes
GLOBEGLO · Mix, Master EQ, Master color, Master out, LFO, Setup
ARPARP · Players (arp, strum, hold), Weave. A step's pad held: its two tricks
SEQSEQ · Steps; K2: Bar. Held on Steps: the keys play, and set what a pad writes
PLAYPLAY · start / stop. SEQ + PLAY on Steps: from this bar
RECREC · REC + PLAY: record live. Held while playing: records while held. Tapped, stopped, on Steps: step record. Tapped twice: a counted-in take: stopped, a bar of clicks then from step 1; playing, from the next bar
NOTE REPEATLFO · tapped: note repeat on / off (lit). Held + pads 1–8: its rate. Held and let go alone: the LFO page
KNOB BANKThe pads: on Steps, cells ↔ live pads (lit); elsewhere, opens Steps
FADER BANKThe faders: T1–T4 ↔ T5–T8 (lit)
< / >The bar before / after, on any page, when let go
< + >DAW mode on / off: the second pressed while the first is held
OCT− / OCT+The keys' octave. A step's pad held: nudge it. SEQ held on Steps: the pattern an octave. Arrange: scenes 1–16 / 17–32. Step record: back / rest
