It boots as the keyboard does: the spider, its sound, the pads. Any key, pad or button skips it.

The panel is as wide as the keyboard's: swipe it sideways.

Play it from your keyboard

Plug a MIDI keyboard into this computer by USB and play the page from it: your SMK-37 as it is now, on its own firmware, works. Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Its keys play the keys, the wheels the wheels; pads sending notes 36–51 on channel 10 play the pads (36–43 the drums). For the rest, Learn a control: click a knob, fader, pad or button here, then move or press yours.

MIDI out

What SPIDER sends shows here (the clock left out). Press < and > together for DAW mode: the pads, knobs, faders, buttons and keys then send their notes and CCs.

Playing it

  • Six tracks: T1 the kit, T2 mono (after the SH-101), T3 poly (after the Juno-106), T4 fm (six operators, sixteen patches: electric pianos, basses, brass, bells, organ...), T5 acid (after the TB-303: velocity 110+ accents, a note held into the next slides), T6 samples (a break). ALGORITHM (K3) picks the track.
  • Sounds: PARA opens the track's sound; K4 (PRESETS) steps through its presets, auditioned as you go. On fm, K5 picks the patch, K6 makes it brighter or duller, K7 its decay longer or shorter, K8 spreads it (detune).
  • The keys play the selected track on every page, Steps too (the pads are the steps there).
  • Pads 9–16 the kit's drums, whatever track is selected. Pads 1–6 the tracks' mutes, 7 the throws (held: the conductor), 8 the effects: a tap opens the delay's page, another the reverb's, another goes back. On SEQ they are the bar's steps; KNOB BANK flips them to live pads.
  • Faders T1–T4; FADER BANK: T5, T6, the effects, the master.
  • NOTE REPEAT tapped: repeat on or off (lit). Held, then a top pad: its rate. Held alone and let go: the LFO page.
  • Kits: a new project starts on ACOUSTIC. With T1 (the kit) selected, PARA opens its Engine page; SCALE opens the list with KIT first: turn K3 for ACOUSTIC, 909 or SYNTH, SCALE again puts it on.
  • > held: the pads fire the throws. < > the bar. Both together: DAW mode.
  • SEQ PLAY plays, SEQ REC held with it records. PATCH is home, held the shift. BT saves.
  • Higher on a key or pad plays softer, lower plays harder. Drag knobs up and down, or scroll over them. Shift-click a button to hold it.
  • Computer keys: pads 1–8 1–8, drums Z X C V B N M ,; notes A–; with W E T Y U O P; Space play, Enter rec, Q note repeat, Shift patch, ← → < >, ↑ ↓ select, - = octave.

Projects you save stay in this browser. It runs at the browser's frame rate, so the boot looks smoother here than on the keyboard.
